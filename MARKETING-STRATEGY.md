# Digital Marketing Strategy for Premier Tech Solution

Complete digital marketing roadmap to drive traffic, generate leads, and grow your HVAC business.

---

## Executive Summary

**Goal:** Generate 50-100 qualified leads per month within 6 months of launch.

**Key Strategies:**
1. **Local SEO:** Dominate local search results
2. **Google Ads:** Immediate lead generation
3. **Social Media:** Build brand awareness and trust
4. **Content Marketing:** Establish expertise and drive organic traffic
5. **Email Marketing:** Nurture leads and retain customers

**Budget Recommendation:**
- **Minimal:** $300-500/month (Google Ads focus)
- **Moderate:** $1,000-2,000/month (Ads + SEO + Social)
- **Aggressive:** $3,000-5,000/month (Full omnichannel approach)

## Strategy 1: Local SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

### Why It Matters
- 97% of people learn more about local companies online
- 46% of all Google searches are local
- Local SEO has highest ROI for service businesses

### Quick Wins (Month 1-2)

#### 1.1 Google Business Profile Optimization ⭐ PRIORITY
- [ ] Claim/verify Google Business Profile
- [ ] Complete 100% of profile information
- [ ] Add 20+ photos (exterior, interior, team, completed work)
- [ ] Choose correct primary category: "HVAC Contractor"
- [ ] Add secondary categories: "Air Conditioning Contractor", "Heating Contractor", "Air Conditioning Repair Service"
- [ ] Write compelling business description (750 characters max)
- [ ] Enable messaging feature
- [ ] Set up service areas
- [ ] Add business attributes (women-owned, veteran-owned, etc. if applicable)

**Action Items:**
- Post weekly Google Business updates (photos, offers, tips)
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
- Add products/services with photos and descriptions

**Expected Result:** Appear in Google Local 3-Pack within 30-60 days

#### 1.2 Get Customer Reviews
- [ ] Ask every satisfied customer for a Google review
- [ ] Create easy process: Send review link via SMS/email
- [ ] Respond to every review (positive and negative)
- [ ] Target: 10 reviews in first month, 50+ within 6 months

**Review Request Template:**
```
Hi [Name], thank you for choosing Premier Tech Solution! 

We'd love to hear about your experience. Would you mind leaving us a quick review?

[Google Review Link]

Your feedback helps us improve and helps other homeowners find great service.

Thanks!
Premier Tech Solution Team
```

#### 1.3 Local Citations & Directories
Submit consistent business information (NAP: Name, Address, Phone) to:

**Essential Directories:**
- Google Business Profile ⭐
- Bing Places for Business
- Apple Maps (via Apple Business Connect)
- Yelp
- Facebook
- HomeAdvisor
- Angi (Angie's List)
- Nextdoor

**HVAC-Specific Directories:**
- ContractorHomequotes.com
- ServiceMagic
- Thumbtack
- Porch
- Houzz (for high-end projects)

**Local/Regional:**
- Chamber of Commerce website
- Local newspaper business directory
- City/county business directory

**Consistency is key:** Ensure NAP is identical across all platforms.

### Long-Term SEO (Month 3-6+)

#### 1.4 On-Page SEO Optimization
- [ ] Optimize page titles for target keywords
  - Home: "[City] HVAC Services | Premier Tech Solution"
  - Services: "Heating, Cooling & HVAC Services in [City] | Premier Tech Solution"
- [ ] Add location keywords throughout content naturally
- [ ] Create city/neighborhood-specific pages (if serving multiple areas)
- [ ] Add schema markup for LocalBusiness, Service, Review

#### 1.5 Content Creation for SEO
Create blog posts/pages targeting local keywords:

**Example Topics:**
- "Best HVAC Companies in [Your City]" (feature yourself)
- "[City] Heating Installation Guide"
- "How Much Does AC Repair Cost in [City]?"
- "Energy Efficiency Tips for [City] Homeowners"

**Keyword Research:**
Use Google Keyword Planner (free) to find:
- "[city] + hvac repair"
- "[city] + furnace installation"
- "[city] + ac repair"
- "[neighborhood] + hvac company"

Target: 2-4 new pages/posts per month

#### 1.6 Build Local Backlinks
Get other local websites to link to you:

**Tactics:**
- Join local chamber of commerce (often includes website link)
- Sponsor local youth sports team (website mention)
- Partner with local real estate agents (link exchange)
- Guest post on local blogs/news sites
- Get listed in "Best HVAC Companies in [City]" roundups

**Quality over quantity:** One link from local newspaper > 10 links from spam sites

## Strategy 2: Google Ads (PPC - Pay-Per-Click)

### Why It Matters
- Immediate visibility (no waiting for SEO)
- Appear above organic results
- Target high-intent keywords
- Only pay when someone clicks

### Campaign Structure

#### 2.1 Search Campaigns (Priority 1)

**Campaign 1: Emergency Services (Highest Priority)**
- Budget: $30-50/day
- Keywords:
  - "emergency hvac repair [city]"
  - "24 hour hvac [city]"
  - "furnace not working [city]"
  - "ac not cooling [city]"
- Ad Copy: "24/7 Emergency HVAC Repair | Licensed & Insured | Call Now"
- Bid Strategy: Maximize conversions

**Campaign 2: Installation Services**
- Budget: $20-40/day
- Keywords:
  - "hvac installation [city]"
  - "new furnace cost [city]"
  - "ac unit replacement [city]"
  - "heat pump installation [city]"
- Ad Copy: "Free HVAC Installation Estimate | Same-Day Service Available"

**Campaign 3: Brand Protection**
- Budget: $5-10/day
- Keywords:
  - "premier tech solution"
  - "premier tech hvac"
  - Competitor names (defensive)

#### 2.2 Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed)
- [ ] Apply for Google Guaranteed badge
- [ ] Pass background check
- [ ] Set weekly budget ($200-500)
- [ ] Only pay per lead (call or message)
- [ ] Appear at very top of search results

**ROI:** Typically best-performing ads for home services.

#### 2.3 Display/Remarketing Campaigns (Optional)
- Target people who visited your website but didn't convert
- Show ads as they browse other websites
- Budget: $10-20/day
- Use before/after images and special offers

### Google Ads Best Practices

**Landing Pages:**
- Send traffic to relevant pages (emergency ads → contact page, installation ads → services page)
- Include phone number prominently
- Have clear call-to-action

**Ad Extensions:**
- Call extension (your phone number)
- Location extension (your service area)
- Sitelink extensions (link to Services, About, Contact)
- Callout extensions ("24/7 Emergency Service", "Free Estimates", "Licensed & Insured")

**Negative Keywords:**
Add these to avoid wasteful clicks:
- "diy"
- "how to"
- "jobs" (unless hiring)
- "salary"
- "training"
- "school"

**Budget Example:**
- Emergency: $1,200/month
- Installation: $600/month
- Brand: $200/month
- **Total:** $2,000/month

**Expected ROI:** $3-8 return for every $1 spent (industry average)

## Strategy 3: Social Media Marketing

### Platform Priority

#### 3.1 Facebook (Highest Priority for HVAC)

**Why Facebook:**
- Largest user base (especially homeowners 35-65)
- Great for local targeting
- Strong ROI for home services

**Content Strategy:**
- Post 3-5 times per week
- Mix of: Educational (40%), Promotional (20%), Behind-the-scenes (20%), Testimonials (20%)

**Content Ideas:**
- Before/after installation photos
- Seasonal maintenance tips
- "Meet the Team" posts
- Customer testimonials (with permission)
- Special offers
- Quick video tips
- Weather-related service reminders

**Facebook Ads:**
- Budget: $300-600/month
- Target: Homeowners in service area, age 30-65
- Objective: Lead generation (contact form)
- Offer: "Free HVAC System Inspection" or "$50 Off First Service"

#### 3.2 Instagram (Optional)

**Best For:** Visual storytelling, younger homeowners

**Content:**
- High-quality photos of completed work
- Short reels: "Day in the life of an HVAC tech"
- Stories: Behind-the-scenes, polls, Q&A
- Hashtags: #HVAC #HVACTech #[YourCity]HVAC #HomeComfort

**Post Frequency:** 2-3 times per week

#### 3.3 YouTube (Long-term Investment)

**Why YouTube:**
- 2nd largest search engine after Google
- Educational content ranks well
- Builds trust and authority

**Video Ideas:**
- "How to change your HVAC filter"
- "Signs you need AC repair"
- "Choosing the right HVAC system for your home"
- Customer testimonials
- Time-lapse of installations

**Starting Point:** 1 video per month, quality over quantity

#### 3.4 Nextdoor (Highly Effective for Local Services)

**Why Nextdoor:**
- Hyper-local (neighborhood-level targeting)
- High trust environment
- Referral-driven community

**Strategy:**
- Claim business profile
- Offer "Nextdoor exclusive" discount (10% off)
- Respond quickly to recommendations requests
- Post helpful seasonal tips
- Encourage satisfied customers to recommend you

### Social Media Management Tools
- **Buffer** or **Hootsuite:** Schedule posts in advance
- **Canva:** Create professional graphics
- **Budget:** $0-30/month for tools

## Strategy 6: Review Management & Reputation

### Review Generation System

**Process:**
1. Complete service → Mark as "satisfied customer"
2. Send thank you text/email with review request
3. Make it easy: Direct link to Google review
4. Follow up once if no response after 3 days
5. Thank everyone who leaves review

**Automation Options:**
- **Podium:** Automates review requests via text ($300/mo)
- **BirdEye:** Comprehensive reputation management ($300-500/mo)
- **Manual:** Use Mailchimp or similar to send automated emails (free-$50/mo)

### Handling Negative Reviews

**Response Template:**
```
Thank you for your feedback, [Name]. We're sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. We take all feedback seriously and would like to make this right. Please call us at [phone] or email [email] so we can discuss this further. We appreciate the opportunity to improve.

- [Your Name], Owner/Manager
Premier Tech Solution
```

**Best Practices:**
- Respond within 24 hours
- Stay professional (never argue)
- Take conversation offline
- Fix the issue if possible
- Follow up after resolution

### Review Velocity Goal
- **Month 1-3:** 2-3 reviews per week (10-15 total)
- **Month 4-6:** 3-5 reviews per week (50+ total)
- **Ongoing:** 5-10 reviews per month

**Why it matters:** Reviews are the #1 local SEO ranking factor.

## Measurement & Analytics

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

**Website Traffic:**
- Goal: 500+ visitors/month by month 6
- Track: Google Analytics
- Monitor: Traffic sources, bounce rate, time on site

**Lead Generation:**
- Goal: 50-100 leads/month by month 6
- Track: Contact form submissions + phone calls
- Cost per lead target: $30-80

**Conversion Rate:**
- Goal: 20-40% of leads convert to customers
- Track: CRM or spreadsheet
- Monitor: Which traffic sources convert best

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
- Formula: Total marketing spend ÷ New customers
- Goal: CAC < 30% of average customer lifetime value
- Example: $500 marketing budget, 10 new customers = $50 CAC

**Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):**
- Formula: Revenue from ads ÷ Ad spend
- Goal: 3:1 minimum, 5:1+ excellent
- Example: $1,000 ad spend, $5,000 revenue = 5:1 ROAS

### Monthly Reporting

Create simple dashboard tracking:
- Website visits (source breakdown)
- Leads generated (by source)
- Conversion rate
- New customers
- Revenue
- Marketing spend
- ROI

**Tools:**
- Google Analytics: Website traffic
- Google Ads: Ad performance
- Facebook Ads Manager: Social media ad performance
- Call tracking software: Phone leads
- Spreadsheet: Manual tracking of conversions and revenue

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Inconsistency:** Posting for 2 weeks then going silent for a month
   - Solution: Use scheduling tools, batch create content

2. **Ignoring negative reviews:** Letting bad reviews sit unanswered
   - Solution: Set up alerts, respond within 24 hours

3. **Wrong targeting:** Advertising to people outside service area
   - Solution: Use location targeting precisely

4. **No tracking:** Not knowing which marketing works
   - Solution: Use UTM codes, call tracking, ask "How did you hear about us?"

5. **Giving up too early:** Expecting results in week 1
   - Reality: SEO takes 3-6 months, ads work faster but need optimization

6. **Trying everything at once:** Spreading too thin
   - Solution: Start with 2-3 channels, master them, then expand
